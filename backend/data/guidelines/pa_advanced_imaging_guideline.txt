GUIDELINE: DEMO PRIOR AUTHORIZATION CRITERIA - ADVANCED IMAGING (MRI AND CT)
VERSION: 1.0
ORGANIZATION: Synthetic Payer Clinical Policy Unit
STATUS: DEMONSTRATION DATA - NOT AN ACTUAL PAYER POLICY

SECTION: Coverage Criteria

Criterion 1: Advanced imaging such as MRI or CT of the spine, joint, or brain
is considered medically necessary when the patient has documented red-flag
symptoms (progressive neurological deficit, suspected malignancy, suspected
infection, or significant trauma) regardless of duration of symptoms.

Criterion 2: For non-emergent back, neck, or joint pain without red-flag
symptoms, advanced imaging is considered medically necessary only after at
least six weeks of documented conservative therapy, such as physical
therapy, activity modification, or anti-inflammatory medication, without
adequate improvement.

Criterion 3: Advanced imaging requested for headache is considered medically
necessary when the patient has new or changed headache pattern, focal
neurological findings, or a personal history of malignancy.

Criterion 4: Repeat advanced imaging of the same body region within twelve
months requires documentation of a significant change in clinical status
since the prior study.

SECTION: Step Therapy and Conservative Treatment Requirements

Requests for musculoskeletal imaging should document prior conservative
treatment attempts, including physical therapy, home exercise programs, or
oral analgesics, along with the duration and outcome of each attempt.

SECTION: Documentation Requirements

Requests must include the requested body region, primary diagnosis code,
duration of symptoms, prior conservative treatments tried, and any red-flag
findings. Missing documentation of conservative treatment duration for
non-emergent requests should be treated as insufficient information rather
than automatic denial.

SECTION: Exclusions

Advanced imaging requested solely for patient reassurance without a
qualifying clinical indication is not considered medically necessary under
this policy.

SECTION: Safety

Determinations under this policy should consider radiation exposure history
for CT requests, contrast allergy history for contrast-enhanced studies, and
renal function where relevant. Any determination generated from this policy
must be reviewed by a qualified clinical reviewer before communication to
the patient or provider.
