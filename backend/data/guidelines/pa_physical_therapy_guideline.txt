GUIDELINE: DEMO PRIOR AUTHORIZATION CRITERIA - EXTENDED PHYSICAL THERAPY
VERSION: 1.0
ORGANIZATION: Synthetic Payer Clinical Policy Unit
STATUS: DEMONSTRATION DATA - NOT AN ACTUAL PAYER POLICY

SECTION: Coverage Criteria

Criterion 1: An initial course of physical therapy of up to twelve visits
over eight weeks is considered medically necessary for a documented
musculoskeletal diagnosis without prior authorization.

Criterion 2: Extension of physical therapy beyond the initial course is
considered medically necessary when the treating therapist documents
measurable functional improvement from the initial course and a specific
remaining functional goal.

Criterion 3: Extension requests without documentation of measurable
improvement from the prior course of therapy are not considered medically
necessary, as continued treatment without progress does not meet medical
necessity criteria.

Criterion 4: Physical therapy for chronic pain maintenance without a
defined functional goal or expectation of continued improvement is not
considered medically necessary beyond the initial course.

SECTION: Step Therapy and Conservative Treatment Requirements

Extended physical therapy requests should document the number of visits
completed in the initial course, the functional outcome measures used, and
the specific goals of continued treatment.

SECTION: Documentation Requirements

Requests must include the diagnosis, number of visits already completed,
functional improvement documented to date, and the specific functional
goals for the requested extension.

SECTION: Exclusions

Physical therapy visits requested beyond what is supported by documented
functional improvement, or for general wellness or maintenance purposes,
are not considered medically necessary under this policy.

SECTION: Safety

Determinations under this policy should consider any relevant comorbidities
that may affect rehabilitation potential or session tolerance. Any
determination generated from this policy must be reviewed by a qualified
clinical reviewer before communication to the patient or provider.
